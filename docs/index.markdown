# Welcome to The AI Alliance GitHub Organization

[The AI Alliance](https://thealliance.ai){:target="_blank"} GitHub organization is the place to discover, use, and contribute to our open-source projects for generative AI.

The AI Alliance is a non-profit organization that supports the success of essential open technology and research projects in the AI community. We are dedicated to the benefit of AI for _all_ of society and _all_ cultures.

## Project Tapestry

<center>
<a href="https://thealliance.ai/projects/tapestry" target="_blank" class="no-glyph">
<img src="https://the-ai-alliance.github.io/tapestry/assets/images/03-tapestry-logo-500x450.png" alt="Tapestry Logo" class="float-right light-border">
</a>
</center>

The AI Alliance [launched](https://thealliance.ai/blog/ai-alliance-launches-project-tapestry-to-build-a-collaborative-foundation-for-open-and-sovereign-ai){:target="_blank"} Project Tapestry to build a collaborative foundation for open and sovereign AI. Project Tapestry is building an open-source platform designed to enable globally-federated development of frontier, open models and other AI tools, while preserving sovereignty, local control, and long-term independence.

**Why this matters:** People will not use models that under perform on their language, culture, and domains of interest. Countries, enterprises, and individuals need AI infrastructure they own and control — with guaranteed data residency, the right to exit the collaboration as they see fit, and the ability to operate independently, for example to build locally-customized models for their needs. Tapestry addresses these goals simultaneously: sovereignty is the performance strategy, not a trade-off against it.

More details are [here]({{site.baseurl}}/open-models-and-data-projects/#project-tapestry) and
the [Project Tapestry GitHub repo](https://github.com/The-AI-Alliance/tapestry){:target="_blank"}.
